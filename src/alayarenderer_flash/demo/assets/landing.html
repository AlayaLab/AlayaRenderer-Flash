Players control gameplay through standard inputs while the physics engine evolves the world state and continuously exports synchronized G-buffers encoding geometry and material properties. Conditioned on these buffers and a text prompt, AlayaRenderer-Flash generates RGB frames at playback rate, enabling real-time visual restyling without altering the underlying physics, scene structure, or gameplay logic.
